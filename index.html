🖱 How to Use It Day to Day

Once the tool is open, the routine is short and repeatable.

  1. Set your shot up. In the game, move the photo-mode camera until the framing, height, and angle look right to you.
  2. Save it as a profile. In the tool, create a new profile and give it a name you will recognise later — "Chapel Low Angle", "Hallway Wide", "Pinhead Portrait".
  3. Capture. Use the capture button to save a screenshot to the folder you selected. The image is saved immediately and clearly named.
  4. Review. Open the built-in review panel. If the shot needs work, adjust and capture again — your profile stays as it was.
  5. Reuse later. Next session, load the profile and you are back to the exact same setup in one click.

💡 Tips for Better Screenshots

Give your profile names that describe the mood, not the location. Locations change as you play; moods are reusable.

Keep one profile deliberately loose and wide, and one deliberately tight. Most great screenshots are a variation of one of those two shapes.

Take several captures of the same setup rather than one. Small changes in lighting and character pose make a big difference between frames.

Back up your profile folder occasionally if you have built up a collection you care about, so a reinstall never costs you your favourite setups.
